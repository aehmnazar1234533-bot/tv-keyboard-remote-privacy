Privacy Policy for TV Keyboard Remote

Last updated: November 19, 2026

TV Keyboard Remote is an Android application developed by Ayham Nizar Jabbar Al-Saadi. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

TV Keyboard Remote is designed to provide remote-control and keyboard functionality for compatible television devices. The application does not intentionally collect or sell your personal information.

Depending on the features you use, the application may process information necessary for connecting your phone to a compatible TV, including network and connection information required for communication between the devices.

2. Advertising

The application may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Advertising services may collect or use certain device information, advertising identifiers, diagnostic information, and other information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Google may use advertising identifiers and related technologies to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application is used only for purposes such as:

  • Connecting the application to compatible TV devices.
  • Providing remote-control and keyboard functionality.
  • Maintaining application functionality and stability.
  • Displaying advertisements through supported advertising services.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers that are necessary for advertising, application functionality, security, or technical services. These providers process information according to their own policies and applicable laws.

5. TV and Network Connections

The application may use local network communication to discover, connect to, and communicate with compatible television devices. Such communication is used to provide the remote-control and keyboard features.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

TV Keyboard Remote does not intentionally maintain a separate online account containing your personal information. Information required temporarily for application functionality may remain on your device according to the application's normal operation. You can remove locally stored application information by uninstalling the application or clearing its application data through Android settings.

8. Children's Privacy

TV Keyboard Remote is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of TV Keyboard Remote, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.

11. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services, including Google AdMob. These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

By using TV Keyboard Remote, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
