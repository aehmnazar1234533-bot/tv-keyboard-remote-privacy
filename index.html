Privacy Policy for TV Keyboard Remote

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TV Keyboard Remote, developed by Ayham Nizar Jabbar Al-Saadi, handles information when you use the application.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep the application simple and transparent. The application is designed primarily to control compatible smart TVs and streaming devices over a local network.

1. Information We Collect

TV Keyboard Remote does not intentionally collect or sell your personal information such as your name, phone number, contacts, messages, photos, or personal files.

Depending on the features you use, the application may process technical information required to establish communication with compatible devices, including local network information, device IP addresses, connection status, and device discovery information.

This information is used to establish and maintain the connection between your phone and the selected TV or compatible device.

2. Local Network Communication

The application may use your local Wi-Fi network to discover and communicate with compatible devices.

Supported devices may include Google TV, Android TV, Samsung smart TVs, LG webOS TVs, Roku devices, Xiaomi smart TVs, and other compatible devices depending on the device model, operating system, firmware, and available network-control protocols.

Some devices may require you to approve pairing or connection requests directly on the TV or device.

Important: Local network communication is used to provide remote-control, keyboard, pairing, discovery, and related functionality.

3. Device Discovery

When you use the device discovery feature, the application may scan your local network for compatible devices. This information is used only to identify devices that may be controlled by the application.

The application does not intentionally upload your local network device list to the developer.

4. Keyboard and Remote Control

The application may send remote-control commands, keyboard input, navigation commands, and other commands to a compatible device when you explicitly use these features.

The application does not intentionally record or store the text you enter through the remote keyboard for advertising or unrelated purposes.

5. Advertising

TV Keyboard Remote may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect or process certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, diagnostics, and interaction information, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable privacy requirements.

The application uses Google's consent and privacy mechanisms where required before requesting personalized advertising.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review: Google Privacy Policy .

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services necessary for advertising, application functionality, diagnostics, or other technical services.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide services used by the application, such as advertising or technical services.

8. Data Storage and Retention

The application does not intentionally maintain a developer-controlled database containing your personal information.

Some connection information or application settings may be stored locally on your device when necessary for the application to function.

You can remove locally stored application data by using the Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to reduce the risk of unauthorized access to information handled by the application. However, no electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

TV Keyboard Remote is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so appropriate action can be considered.

11. Your Choices

You can stop using the application or uninstall it at any time. You can also control certain Android permissions through your device settings.

Where applicable, advertising consent choices can be managed through the privacy and consent options provided by the application.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
